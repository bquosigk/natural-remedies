How evidence is rated

  • Good evidence Systematic reviews or several good trials, or the standard of care.
  • Some evidence At least one decent trial, or promising but limited or mixed results.
  • Traditional use only No meaningful human research yet, or research that didn’t show benefit. That doesn’t mean it can’t comfort, only that it hasn’t been shown.
