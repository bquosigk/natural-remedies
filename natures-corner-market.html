Nature’s Corner Market

A family-owned natural-foods market and supplement store in downtown Cartersville, and what it publicly shares about remedies.

Which store: Nature’s Corner Market, founded in 2008 by Jill Rowland (a naturopath) and Doug Rowland. The Cartersville location is at 105 South Dixie Avenue, Cartersville, GA 30120, phone (470) 315-4037, open Mon–Sat 9am–7pm and Sun 10am–6pm (per its website). Its other stores are in Woodstock (200 Parkbrooke Dr) and Jasper (Natural Market Place).

naturescornermarket.com · Facebook · Instagram · YouTube

What they publish: the store doesn’t have a remedy blog or symptom guide. Its public advice comes mainly from six YouTube videos (owner interviews, a supplement-brand segment, and a 2021 guest talk), its café juice menu, its list of local vendors, and customer reviews that mention staff recommendations. Everything here is quoted or summarized from those sources, with links. Video quotes come from YouTube’s auto-generated transcripts, lightly cleaned up; bracketed words correct obvious transcription errors. Some videos were recorded at the Woodstock or Marietta stores, so ask the Cartersville staff what they carry and recommend today.

Each item gets the same treatment as the rest of the guide: a research rating with real sources, plus kid-safety and interaction cautions. Inclusion is not an endorsement, and a rating describes the evidence for the ingredient, not the store. Left out: supplements with no symptom link (cholesterol, eye pressure, women’s health), detox and weight-loss juice cleanses, foot baths, sauna, lab testing, and the COVID, vaccine, and prescription-drug content of the 2021 guest talk.
