Most pediatric practices and insurance plans offer a 24-hour nurse line. It is worth writing yours down and keeping it with this guide.

Foraging note

American elder (Sambucus canadensis) grows wild along Georgia roadsides and creeks. It is easily confused with toxic look-alikes such as water hemlock (the flower clusters look similar) and pokeweed (dark berries). Only harvest when certain, use only fully ripe dark-purple berries with stems removed, and always cook them.
